🏆 Hold My Bear Leaderboard Guide

Welcome to the Hold My Bear Leaderboard — where your skills translate into real USDC rewards, paid every 24h in USDC on the Coreum chain! This guide explains how the leaderboard works, how prizes are distributed, and how to boost your chances of winning big by purchasing extra lives.

⚠️ Important Note

Before you start playing, make sure your Cosmo Bear Wallet is connected!

Why? Rewards will be automatically distributed to your wallet after each daily leaderboard reset. If your wallet isn’t connected, you won’t be eligible to receive your prize!

🎯 How It Works

1. Daily Leaderboard Reset:

  • The leaderboard resets every 24 hours.
  • Compete throughout the day by playing Hold My Bear as many times as possible.

2. Earning Profit:

  • Your daily profit total is the sum of all your profitable tries throughout the day.
  • Example: If you scored +17%, +69%, and +132% in three separate tries, your total profit (score) will be +218%.
  • The more successful tries you make, the higher your rank on the leaderboard!

3. Rewards Distribution:

  • At the end of each 24-hour cycle, the top 30 players share the USDC prize pool.

💰 Rewards Breakdown

  • Current Leaders: Track your current rank in real-time and see your potential reward update dynamically as you play.
  • Previous Winners: Check the previous day's winners to see if you made the list or find inspiration from top players!

🔄 Buy Extra Lives — Boost Your Winning Chances!

Here's the ultimate pro tip for serious competitors: More Lives = More Chances to Win!

When you run out of lives, your game isn't over! You can buy additional lives using Telegram Stars and keep playing to improve your score. Every extra try increases your daily profit total and your chance to climb higher on the leaderboard.

Why Buy Extra Lives?

  • ✔ Stay in the Game: Don't stop playing when your initial lives run out.
  • ✔ Higher Profit Total: Each try adds to your total profit, boosting your rank.
  • ✔ Better Rewards: The higher your position, the bigger your potential USDC payout!

How to Buy Lives:

1. Tap the Buy Lives button in the game.

2. Choose how many lives you want to purchase.

3. If you don't have Telegram Stars, you will be offered to buy them directly from Telegram first.

4. Confirm & Pay — You're back in the game with more chances to win big!
